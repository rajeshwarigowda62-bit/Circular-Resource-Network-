One facility’s waste is another facility’s resource.

CRN is a registry where industries list the heat, water, brine and materials they have to spare, alongside the facilities that need them. Phase 1 puts that information in one place.

Why a shared registry

Many resources are thrown away on one side of a fence while the other side pays for the same thing. The gap is usually missing information, not missing technology.

Surplus on one side
A data center vents warm air, a desalination plant discharges brine, a factory pays to dispose of offcuts.
Demand on the other
A greenhouse buys fuel for heat, a salt works needs brine, a recycler looks for feedstock.
What CRN adds
One place to record both sides, so a connection can be found instead of guessed.

How it works

Three steps, from a facility’s first record to the data that matching will use.

  1. Register sources

    Record waste heat, water, brine or reusable materials a facility can offer.

  2. Register demand

    Record the facilities that need a particular resource, and how much.

  3. Prepare for matching Phase 2

    Sources and consumers will be compared for compatibility in the next phase.
